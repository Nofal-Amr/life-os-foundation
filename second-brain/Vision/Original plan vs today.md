# Original plan vs today

How the app built so far lines up with [[Original plan - Real-life RPG operating system|the original plan]].

> [!important] The big difference
> The plan is **RPG-first**: the character and the game are the product. The app grew **calm-first**: a Serious skin by default, with RPG as an opt-in skin, and a rule that every number comes from something logged ([[Product rules]]). Several parts of the plan (Energy and Focus meters, loot chances, critical successes, future projections) need a decision on how they fit that rule. See [[#Conflicts to decide]].

Things built that the plan didn't have: prayers and reminders, Samsung Health, electricity tiers, Keep-style notes, offline mode, the Android app, a calm Serious skin.

## Conflicts to decide

1. **RPG-first or calm-first?** Is the game the product (plan), or a skin on a calm life tracker (today)?
2. **Energy and Focus are estimates.** "85 energy" isn't logged data. Options: you rate energy and focus yourself; they're estimated from logged sleep, work and rest, with the formula shown; or leave them out.
3. **Chance-based rewards** (loot chests, critical success) are random by design. That's exciting, but random rewards can pull people back into the app, which the plan itself warns against in section 40.
4. **Weighted Life Level**: who sets the weights? Your priorities in setup, or fixed?
5. **Future simulator**: projections must show their assumptions ("if you keep saving 1,200 a month…") to stay honest.
6. **Prayer's place**: the plan has no Spirit attribute; the app does.
